Fully Synthetic Motor Oil

5W-40

API SN Plus / SN

Fully Synthetic • synTECH+

Fully-synthetic engine oil for petrol, LPG and light diesel engines in passenger cars, including cars equipped with catalytic converters and direct injection systems.

Application

Fully-synthetic engine oil for petrol, LPG and light diesel engines in passenger cars. Can be used in cars equipped with catalytic converters and direct injection systems. Manufactured with state-of-the-art additives in selected fully synthetic base oils, offering high protection in extreme conditions of temperature and pressure.

Key Features

  • Superior oxidation and thermal stability ensuring long life and remain stay in grade
  • Superior high temperature and low temperature properties ensuring smooth service life
  • Enhanced emission control system protection
  • Protection of engines operating on ethanol mix gasoline
  • Excellent high temperature deposit protection of pistons and turbochargers
  • Superior control on sludge and varnish
  • Improved fuel economy due to special friction modifiers

Recommended For

Petrol, LPG and light diesel passenger car engines Vehicles with catalytic converters and direct injection systems. Same feature set as the 5W-30 SN Plus FS grade; differs in viscosity (KV@100°C 14.5 cSt vs 11.6 cSt; VI 151 vs 145), giving thicker film at operating temperature.
